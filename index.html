    What you are seeing

    Starting

    1. Committee commits the batch. 3 of 4 nodes sign the digest of the encrypted orders. Nobody has read them.
    2. Certificate goes to the keypers. Each keyper checks the 3 signatures; it never sees order contents.
    3. Keypers send their shares. One share each per batch, however many orders it holds. Shares also gossip around the ring.
    4. The third share opens the batch. Shares that arrive later are not needed, so distant keypers add safety, not delay.
    5. Decrypt and clear. Every committee node decrypts the batch and clears each market at one price.
